# TensoraMax Studio: UI/UX Guidelines - Video Editor

The Video Editor module in TensoraMax Studio is designed to provide a comprehensive and intuitive platform for video creation and post-production, significantly enhanced by TXB's AI capabilities. The UI/UX guidelines for the Video Editor focus on streamlining complex editing tasks, fostering creativity, and ensuring a smooth workflow for users of all skill levels.

## Layout and Structure

The Video Editor's layout is optimized for visual content, featuring a prominent preview area, a multi-track timeline, and panels for media assets, effects, and TXB's AI suggestions. The design prioritizes clear visual hierarchy and easy access to frequently used tools.

### 1. Preview Panel

*   **Purpose:** Display real-time playback of the video project.
*   **Features:** Playback controls (play, pause, scrub), resolution settings, and full-screen mode.
*   **Clarity:** Ensure high-quality video rendering and accurate color representation.

### 2. Multi-Track Timeline

*   **Purpose:** Arrange and edit video, audio, and graphic tracks.
*   **Features:** Intuitive drag-and-drop functionality, precise trimming and cutting tools, and visual representation of clips and transitions.
*   **Scalability:** Support for multiple video and audio tracks, allowing for complex compositions.

### 3. Media Library

*   **Purpose:** Manage and organize all imported media assets (video clips, images, audio files).
*   **Features:** Search and filter capabilities, asset tagging, and quick preview options.
*   **TXB Integration:** AI-powered asset tagging, content suggestions, and duplicate detection.

### 4. Effects and Transitions Panel

*   **Purpose:** Apply visual effects, color corrections, and transitions to video clips.
*   **Features:** Categorized effects library, customizable parameters, and real-time preview of applied effects.
*   **Discoverability:** Make a wide range of effects easily discoverable and applicable.

### 5. TXB Assistant Panel

*   **Purpose:** Provide AI-driven suggestions for editing, content generation, and optimization.
*   **Features:** Proactive scene analysis, scriptwriting assistance, voiceover generation, and recommendations for music and sound effects.
*   **Workflow Enhancement:** TXB's suggestions should seamlessly integrate into the editing process, offering creative boosts without disrupting flow.

## Interaction Design

Interactions within the Video Editor are designed to be highly visual and responsive, allowing for precise control over media elements. Contextual menus, keyboard shortcuts, and intuitive manipulation of timeline elements are key.

### 1. Timeline Interaction

*   **Precise Editing:** Tools for frame-accurate trimming, splitting, and merging of clips.
*   **Layer Management:** Easy reordering and grouping of tracks for complex projects.
*   **Audio Control:** Independent volume control, audio ducking, and noise reduction features.

### 2. TXB Integration

*   **Automated Scene Detection:** TXB can automatically identify scene changes and suggest cuts.
*   **Smart Content Generation:** Users can prompt TXB to generate script ideas or voiceovers based on video content.
*   **Style Transfer:** AI-powered suggestions for visual styles or color grading based on user preferences or reference videos.

### 3. Export and Sharing

*   **Preset Management:** Offer a range of export presets for various platforms and resolutions.
*   **Custom Export Settings:** Allow advanced users to fine-tune export parameters.
*   **Direct Sharing:** Integrate with popular social media and video hosting platforms for direct sharing.

By adhering to these guidelines, the Video Editor in TensoraMax Studio aims to be a powerful, intelligent, and user-friendly environment for all video production needs.
